Privacy policy

The Clockmaker’s Postcards is a single-player detective game from Parashar Shah. You can play without creating an account with us.

Your game stays on your device

The game stores chapter progress, collected clues and preferences locally. It does not upload your notebook or game progress to our servers. We do not operate a gameplay backend, advertising service or analytics service for this app.

Purchases

Apple processes the optional Full Adventure purchase. The app uses Apple’s StoreKit services through Unity In-App Purchasing to check purchase ownership, deliver the unlock and restore it. We do not receive your payment-card details. Purchase information is handled on the device and by Apple; the app does not send receipts to a developer-operated server. Apple’s handling of purchase and account data is governed by Apple’s privacy policy.

No advertising or tracking

The app has no advertising, no cross-app tracking and no third-party analytics collection. It does not request access to your location, contacts, camera or microphone.

Support and this website

If you open a support issue, GitHub processes what you choose to submit. Issues are public; avoid personal or sensitive information. This website is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may process standard connection information under GitHub’s privacy statement. The website does not embed analytics or advertising scripts.

Control and deletion

You can remove locally stored game data by deleting the app and its data through your device’s settings. Apple purchase records remain managed by Apple. There is no developer account to delete.

Questions and changes

For privacy questions, use the support page without posting private information. We will update this page if the app’s data practices change.
