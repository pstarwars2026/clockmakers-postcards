Support

For a bug report or gameplay question, open a support request on GitHub. Include your device model, operating-system version, app version, chapter and what happened. GitHub Issues are public: do not post passwords, purchase receipts, account details or other private information.

Getting started

Tap a place to walk there. Tap a clue marker to investigate, then use Notebook to review evidence and Deduce to solve the case. The question-mark button offers hints. Pinch on a touchscreen, or scroll with a mouse, to adjust your view.

Full Adventure and restore

The opening case in Paris, Venice and Murano is free. The one-time Full Adventure purchase unlocks chapters 3–12. Open Settings → Full Adventure to purchase or choose Restore Purchases. Use the Apple Account that originally purchased the unlock and an internet connection. Apple handles billing and refund requests.

Saved discoveries

Progress is saved locally and resumes when you return. Purchase ownership can be restored on supported devices with the same Apple Account; chapter progress does not sync between devices. Deleting the app may remove its local progress.

Sound and motion

Settings includes separate music and effects volume controls, plus reduced travel animation. If sound is missing, also check your device volume and audio output.

Release status

The native app is being prepared for App Store release. This page does not indicate that Apple has approved or published it.
